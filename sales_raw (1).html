monthly_summary = df.groupby('Month')['Sales'].sum()

plt.ylabel("Sales")

    plt.text(x, y, f"{y:.0f}", ha='center', va='bottom')

plt.show()

Insight 1 — Monthly Trend¶

Sales show noticeable variation across months, indicating seasonal demand patterns. Certain months consistently generate higher revenue, suggesting opportunities for targeted promotions and inventory planning during peak periods.

Insight 2 — Product Performance¶

A small group of products contributes a significant portion of total sales. This indicates a Pareto effect (80/20 rule), and the business should prioritize stocking, marketing, and bundling strategies for these top-performing products.

Insight 3 — Regional Performance¶

Sales are uneven across regions, with some regions clearly outperforming others. Underperforming regions may require focused marketing campaigns, pricing adjustments, or distribution improvements to boost revenue.
