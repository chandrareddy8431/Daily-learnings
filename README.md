# 🚀 Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project analyzes business sales performance using Power BI to uncover actionable insights that support data-driven decision making.

The dashboard provides a **comprehensive view of sales, profit, and regional performance**, enabling stakeholders to quickly identify growth opportunities and risk areas.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Analyze overall sales and profitability  
- Track monthly sales trends  
- Compare regional performance  
- Identify top-performing product categories  
- Highlight high-revenue products  
- Build an interactive business dashboard  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Data visualization & dashboarding  
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data preprocessing  
- **DAX** – KPI calculations  

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned before analysis to ensure accuracy.

**Steps performed:**

- ✔ Removed duplicate records  
- ✔ Handled missing/null values  
- ✔ Fixed incorrect data types  
- ✔ Standardized category and region fields  
- ✔ Validated numeric columns (Sales, Profit, Quantity)  

✅ This ensured reliable and high-quality insights.

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals

### 🔹 Sales by Region (Bar Chart)

- West region leads with the highest sales (~0.73M)  
- East follows closely  
- South shows the lowest performance  

📌 **Business Value:** Helps identify strong and weak markets.

---

### 🔹 Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)

- Sales fluctuate across the year  
- Strong peak observed in **October (~0.31M)**  
- Moderate dip around **February–April**

📌 **Business Value:** Reveals seasonality and planning opportunities.

---

### 🔹 Profit by Category (Bar Chart)

- 🥇 Technology generates the highest profit (~145K)  
- 🥈 Office Supplies performs moderately (~122K)

📌 **Business Value:** Highlights profitability gaps across categories.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

🔹 The **West region** is the strongest revenue contributor.  

🔹 **Technology category** dominates overall profitability.  

🔹 **Furniture category shows very low profit**, indicating margin issues.  

🔹 Sales show **clear seasonality with Q4 peak**.  

🔹 Revenue is concentrated among a few high-performing products.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

✅ **Improve South region performance**  
- Launch targeted marketing campaigns  
- Strengthen distribution channels  

✅ **Optimize Furniture category profitability**

- Negotiate supplier costs  
- Adjust pricing where possible
